Intent

The staggered recipe with one field flipped: "loop": false. Finite timelines clamp — a row at position duration has no next tick, so its record is skipped: the position stays, no effect folds. Rows seeded near the end park immediately while younger rows keep moving; within 64 frames the entire crowd stands at the end state. Watch the skipped aggregate climb and the strip freeze from the tail forward — the row-level completion model of the data-authored API made visible.
