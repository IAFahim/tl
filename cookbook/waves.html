Recipe 3 — waves of rows through blended clip windows

all recipes

Intent

256 rows split into eight waves, and two clips whose windows overlap over ticks 24..39. Where both clips cover a tick, the baked slot stores both payloads and the runtime asks the track's Blend for the interpolated clip — the effect per tick is neither +1 nor -2 but the blend of the two. In the strip the blend region shows up as the band where colors move smoothly instead of stepping; per-row effects drift while inside it. This is the deterministic cross-fade semantics of Tl.IBlend<TClip>, authored entirely as data.
