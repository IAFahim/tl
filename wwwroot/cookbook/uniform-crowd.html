Recipe 1 — a uniform crowd on a looping timeline

all recipes

Intent

The smallest moving picture tl can draw: one looping 64-tick timeline, one track (Play.ScaleTrack) carrying a single clip (Play.AmountClip amount 1 over ticks 0..31), and 96 rows that all start at position 0. Because every position is equal, the gather router sees one long run and the whole crowd advances as a block — watch the strip move in lockstep and the effect total grow by 96 per tick inside the clip window, then hold during the 32..39 gap and fall by 2 per row after tick 40.
